Development of a mobile tracking and outdoor navigation application tailored for hiking and cycling tourism networks.

Designed and implemented real-time GPS tracking and interactive mapping features, allowing users to navigate pre-defined trails with live tracking for elapsed time, distance covered, and elevation profiles. Integrated contextual point-of-interest (POI) markers for local services and built an emergency/issue reporting module to send real-time incident updates from active routes.
