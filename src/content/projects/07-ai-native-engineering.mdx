I established an AI-native engineering workflow around a shared, evolving source of project knowledge. The case study focuses on how I develop software and validate decisions.

**Requirements and decisions.** I turn conversations into structured requirements, record assumptions and open questions, and maintain product and architecture documentation alongside implementation.

**Context and implementation.** I keep agent instructions, technical decisions, implementation status and meeting summaries consistent, then guide coding agents through incremental changes with explicit scope and acceptance criteria.

**Engineering review.** I review technical choices and generated code, check behavior against requirements, investigate issues and update the shared context as the implementation evolves. AI supports execution; responsibility for architecture, correctness and maintainability remains mine.
